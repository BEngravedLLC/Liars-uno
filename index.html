📖 Liar's UNO rules

Goal

Be first to get rid of all your cards.

Two kinds of cards

Regular cards are always played face up. They must match the pile by color, number or symbol.

Liar's cards have white circles in the corners, a dashed white frame and a little "LIAR'S" tag. They're always played face down, and you say what they are.

Playing a Liar's card

If it matches, tell the truth. If it doesn't, lie, but make it believable: it has to be a card of the pile's color, the same number or symbol, or a Wild Draw 4.

Anyone can Call bluff! before time runs out:

  • Caught lying? You take the card back and draw 1.
  • Telling the truth? The person who called you draws 1.
  • Nobody calls it? It counts as whatever you said, and its action happens, even if you lied.

Can't play?

Draw 1. If it can be played, you may play it right away, otherwise your turn ends. You can also choose to draw even if you could play.

Action cards (all are Liar's cards)

  • Skip: next player loses their turn.
  • Reverse: flips direction (with 2 players it works like a Skip).
  • Draw Two: next player must stack or take 2 (see below).
  • Wild Draw 4: playable on anything. You pick the color, and the next player must stack or take 4.

🔥 Stacking (house rule)

When a +2 or +4 hits you, you can stack another +2 or +4 on it (any color) and pass the whole pile to the next player. Or hit Take to draw them all and lose your turn.

Stacks are played face down, so you can bluff a stack with any Liar's card. Get caught and you eat the whole stack plus 1. Call a real stack a bluff and you draw 1, and the stack keeps going.

Wild Liar's Challenge (played face up)

You pick a color. Everyone else puts one card face down and says it's that color (wilds don't count). No card of that color? Put anything down and bluff.

You then flip players' cards one at a time. Liars take their card back and draw 1. Once you flip someone who told the truth (or you stop), that player and everyone you didn't flip get to discard their card. Going out this way wins, even for several players at once.

Special numbers

  • 7 Swap: swap hands with a player you choose.
  • 0 Pass: everyone passes their hand to the next player.
  • If a 7 or 0 gets challenged and was true, the penalty card is drawn after the swap.

UNO!

Hit UNO! when you're down to one card (you can hit it with 2 cards on your turn, right before you play). If someone catches you first with the 🎯 Catch button before the next player moves, you draw 2.

Winning & scoring

If your last card is face down and nobody catches a lie, you win the hand. You score points for what's left in everyone else's hand: numbers = face value, Skip/Reverse/Draw Two = 20, wilds = 50. First to 500 wins the match.
